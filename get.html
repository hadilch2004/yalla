What you can do with Yalla

🗺️

See the campus live

A map of the Al Akhawayn campus shows where hangouts are happening right now: the library, the cafeteria, the sports field and more.

🎲

Start or join hangouts

Post a plan in a few taps, see what your friends are joining and get suggestions based on what you like.

💬

Chat with the group

Every hangout has its own group chat, so everyone knows where to meet.

💛

Add besties

Mutual besties can see each other on the map, and you can walk a route straight to them.

📣

Clubs and communities

Clubs run a community page, post events, share photos and team up with other clubs.

👻

You control your privacy

Ghost mode is the default. Nobody sees where you are until you choose, and sharing stops by itself after the time you pick.

Install Yalla on your phone

Yalla is not in the App Store or Google Play. You install it straight from this website, and it then opens full screen with its own icon, like any other app.

📱

iPhone

  1. Open yalla-afk.pages.dev in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
🤖

Android

  1. Open yalla-afk.pages.dev in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install on the banner, or open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).
💻

On a computer?

Just open Yalla in your browser. Or scan this code with your phone to open it there.

Questions

Is Yalla free?

Yes. Creating an account and using the app is free.

Who can see where I am?

Only the besties you both added, and only if you turn sharing on. Ghost mode is the default, you can turn sharing off at any moment, and a timer stops it automatically.

Do I need an Al Akhawayn email?

No, you can sign up with any email address. The map is built around the Al Akhawayn campus in Ifrane.

Can my club use Yalla?

Yes. Choose "We're a club" when you sign up to create events, run a community and follow up with your members. New clubs are checked by the Yalla team before they are marked as verified.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Me tab and tap Delete my account. Your profile and your data are removed.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes, open the demo. It uses sample data and nothing is saved on a server.
